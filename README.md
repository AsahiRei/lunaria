# 🌙 Lunaria

> **Learn Brighter Under The Moonlight.**

Lunaria is an **offline AI-powered study reviewer app** designed to help students turn their learning materials into effective study resources.

Simply import a **PDF**, and Lunaria can analyze the material and generate a structured reviewer and quiz to help you understand and retain the topic more effectively.

## Features

- **PDF to Reviewer**
  - Import PDF study materials.
  - Generate structured and easy-to-understand reviewers using AI.

- **AI-Generated Quizzes**
  - Generate quizzes based on your study materials.
  - Test your understanding and reinforce what you've learned.

- **Study Topics**
  - Organize your reviewers and quizzes by topic.
  - Easily return to previously generated study materials.

- **Study Progress**
  - Track your progress across different topics.
  - Monitor your learning and quiz performance.

- **Offline AI**
  - Uses on-device AI for generating study materials.
  - Designed to work without requiring constant internet access.

- **Student-Focused UI**
  - Clean and calming interface designed for studying.
  - Moonlight-inspired visual design.

## Goal

Lunaria aims to make studying easier by transforming long and difficult-to-review
learning materials into **simple, interactive, and personalized study resources**.

Instead of manually creating reviewers and quizzes, students can let Lunaria
do the work and focus more on actually learning.
